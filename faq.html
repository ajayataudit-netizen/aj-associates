Frequently Asked Questions

1. What documents are required to file my ITR?

PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16, bank statements, and investment proofs (80C, 80D).

2. When is the last date to file GST returns?

GSTR-3B is generally due by the 20th of the succeeding month, and GSTR-1 by the 11th.

3. How do I register a Private Limited Company or LLP?

We handle DSC/DIN acquisition, SPICe+ name approval, MOA/AOA drafting, and MCA incorporation.

4. What should I do if I receive an Income Tax scrutiny notice?

Forward it immediately to us. We will review the grounds and file a legal response.
